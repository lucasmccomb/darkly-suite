Q1 Marketing Strategy
Darkly Suite - January 2026
Executive Summary

This document outlines the Q1 2026 marketing strategy for the Darkly Suite family of Chrome extensions. Our primary goal is to expand user adoption across all three products (Darkly for Gmail, Darkly for Sheets, and Darkly for Docs) while driving conversions to the unified Darkly Suite bundle. With growing demand for dark mode experiences across productivity tools, we are positioned to capture significant market share in the Chrome Web Store productivity category.

Key performance indicators for Q1 include reaching 10,000 weekly active users across all extensions, achieving a 4.5+ star average rating on the Chrome Web Store, and converting 15% of free users to paid subscribers through targeted campaigns and product improvements.

Target Audience

Our primary audience consists of knowledge workers, developers, and digital professionals who spend 4+ hours daily in Google Workspace applications. Secondary audiences include students, accessibility-focused users who benefit from reduced eye strain, and enterprise teams seeking consistent dark mode theming across their productivity stack. Research indicates that 73% of users prefer dark mode when available, with the highest adoption among 25-44 year-old professionals.

Key Initiatives
  • Launch coordinated Chrome Web Store listing updates with unified branding and cross-promotion banners linking all Darkly extensions
  • Develop a referral program offering 30-day free trials of Darkly Suite for users who share the extension with colleagues
  • Create video tutorials and comparison screenshots demonstrating dark mode benefits across Gmail, Sheets, and Docs workflows
  • Partner with productivity-focused YouTube channels and newsletter creators for sponsored reviews and feature highlights
  • Implement in-extension onboarding flows that guide new users through theme customization and introduce the Suite bundle upgrade path
